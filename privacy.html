Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

The short version. This website itself collects very little. If you ask us to call you back, we take your name and number. If you enrol, you leave this site and give your details directly to National Telehealth Providers, who deliver the care and take the payment. We never see your card details, and we do not sell your information.

Who we are

Nuvantu Health markets and refers people to a virtual primary care membership. The membership itself, the clinical care, the medical records and the billing are provided by National Telehealth Providers and the licensed clinicians working with them. We are not a medical provider and we do not practise medicine.

This matters for privacy because it means there are two organisations involved, and different information sits with each of us. This policy covers ours. Theirs is governed by their own privacy notice and, where it applies to your medical records, by health privacy law.

What this website collects

There is one form on this site: the call-back request. If you fill it in, we receive the first name, telephone number and, if you choose to give it, email address that you type. Nothing else on the page asks you for anything.

Like most websites, we also receive ordinary technical information automatically — your IP address, browser and device type, the page you arrived from, and which advertisement you clicked. We use that to work out which of our adverts are worth running. It does not tell us who you are.

What we do not collect

  • Card and payment details. These are entered on National Telehealth Providers' checkout, not here. We never see them and never hold them.
  • Your medical information. The health questions are asked during their intake, after you have left this site. Your answers go to them and to the clinicians treating you. They do not come back to us.

What we do with the call-back details

We use them for exactly what you asked: to telephone or text you back about the membership and answer your questions. We may contact you more than once if we cannot reach you. If you tell us to stop, we stop, and you can say so on any call or text.

If you decide to join, we will point you to the enrolment page. We do not enrol you ourselves and we cannot sign you up without you completing their checkout.

Who we share it with

We share what you give us with National Telehealth Providers where it is needed to set up or support your membership, and with the service providers who run our website, telephone and record-keeping systems.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not pass it to unrelated advertisers or list brokers.

How we are paid

We are paid by National Telehealth Providers when someone we refer becomes a member. That means we have a financial interest in you joining, and you should read what we say about the membership with that in mind. What we are paid does not change your price, and it does not affect the care you receive.

Advertising and measurement

We advertise on platforms such as Meta. Those platforms may set cookies or similar identifiers to tell us that an advert led to a visit or an enrolment. We use this to judge which adverts work. You can limit this through your browser settings and through the ad preference controls of the platform you came from.

How long we keep it

We keep call-back enquiries for as long as needed to deal with the enquiry and to meet our record-keeping obligations, after which they are deleted. Technical and advertising logs are kept for a shorter period.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to ask for a copy, to have it corrected or deleted, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell personal information. You will not be treated differently for exercising any of these rights.

Requests about your medical records or your membership billing need to go to National Telehealth Providers, because we do not hold either.

Children

This service is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18 through this website.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will appear here with a new date at the top.
