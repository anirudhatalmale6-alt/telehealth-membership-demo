Questions people actually ask

Is this insurance?

No, and it is not a replacement for it. This is a membership for routine and urgent care without per-visit bills. It will not cover a hospital stay or surgery. Plenty of members keep a high-deductible insurance plan for emergencies and use this for everything else.

Can I get prescriptions?

Yes, when a provider decides one is appropriate. It goes to the pharmacy you already use. Over a thousand common medications are available at no cost through participating local pharmacies. Controlled substances are not prescribed.

Do I see the same doctor each time?

For your primary care, yes. That is the point of the membership rather than a walk-in service. Urgent care at 2am will be whoever is on shift, which is how urgent care works everywhere.

What if I want to cancel?

Cancel any time from your account. Monthly members stop at the end of the current month. Annual members can cancel and keep access for the rest of the term already paid for.

Is my family covered?

Not on this plan. There is a family membership that covers up to seven additional people on one subscription — ask us and we will send the details.

Not ready to sign up?

Leave your details and someone will call you back to answer whatever is holding you up. No obligation, and we will not hound you.
